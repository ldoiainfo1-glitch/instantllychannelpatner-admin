Payment Plans Configuration

Edit payment tiers for each position level. Changes will reflect immediately on the channel partner application page.

How it works:
  • Pay (₹): Amount the partner pays to join
  • Profit (₹): Earnings potential from the position
  • Credits: Digital credits assigned to the partner
  • Each position level can have multiple payment options
  • Click "Save All Changes" to update the channel partner application form
